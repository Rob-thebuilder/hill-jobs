More paths into Hill

  • High school students: the Utah Aerospace Pathway lets juniors and seniors earn an aerospace manufacturing certificate. It's listed with other programs under More ways to find Hill jobs.
  • Leaving the military: DoD SkillBridge internships are listed under More ways to find Hill jobs, and the base's Military & Family Readiness Center is on the Resume Resources tab.
  • Students and recent grads: paid federal Pathways internships at Hill are also under More ways to find Hill jobs.
  • Already trained? Read how hiring at Hill works, including how WG trades pay works, then search the Employer Directory.

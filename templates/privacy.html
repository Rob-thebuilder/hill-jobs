Privacy

Short version: browsing Work At Hill collects nothing about you. If you sign up for the weekly email, your email address and any saved searches are kept by our email service, Buttondown, and used only to send you that email.

Browsing the site

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, including which links you click.
  • The page loads nothing from other companies. Searches and filters run in your browser and are never sent anywhere.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy.

The weekly email (optional)

  • What's kept: your email address, and the search terms you ask to be alerted about (for example "sheet metal"). Nothing else.
  • Who keeps it: Buttondown, the email service that stores the list and sends the email. Work At Hill never sells, rents or shares your address.
  • Confirming: after you sign up, you'll get an email asking you to confirm. Nothing is sent until you do.
  • What you get: at most one email a week, with new jobs near Hill, upcoming events and new matches for your saved searches.
  • Stopping: every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops all emails.
  • Deleting your data: to have your address and saved searches deleted, write to the address below or reply to any email.

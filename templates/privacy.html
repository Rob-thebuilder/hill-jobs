Browsing the site

  • Google Analytics: the site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies, to count visits and see which employer links get clicked. It records things like the pages you view (including words you search for on the site, which show up in the page address), how you got here, and your general area and type of device. It doesn't tell us your name or email address.
  • Never sold: Work At Hill never sells personal information. Google handles analytics data under its own privacy policy.
  • Opting out: install Google's Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or block cookies in your browser. The site works the same either way.
  • No accounts. Searches and filters run in your browser, and apart from the page address that Google Analytics sees, they aren't sent anywhere.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy.

Children

Work At Hill is meant for adults looking for work. It doesn't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us their details, tell us and we'll delete them.

Who's behind this

Hi, I'm Rob Smith. I've been in the aviation maintenance world for 10+ years, and one thing never changes: finding a job at Hill is harder than it should be. There are dozens of contractors, each with its own careers site, plus USAJOBS, job fairs, training programs and resume help, all scattered across the internet.

So I built Work At Hill to pull it all into one place. It's local, independent and free, for job seekers and for employers.

How listings work

  • Free for job seekers, always. Every employer is listed free, sorted A–Z, and nobody can pay to rank higher in the directory. If the site carries sponsors, they're clearly labeled and kept separate from the listings.
  • Straight to the source. Every "View jobs" link goes to the employer's own careers site or USAJOBS. Work At Hill never takes applications or collects resumes.
  • Only employers with people at Hill. Each of the {{count}} employers listed has evidence of a workforce on or near the base.
  • Kept current. Federal job counts refresh daily from USAJOBS, every link is checked weekly, and corrections are made by hand.
  • Independent. Work At Hill isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Air Force, the Department of Defense or any listed employer.

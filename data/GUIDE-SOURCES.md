## 3. Security clearances
| Claim | Source | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| Levels include Confidential, Secret, Top Secret (and SCI) | https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CFR-2025-title32-vol1/CFR-2025-title32-vol1-sec117-10 (32 CFR 117.10, the contractor security rules) | "information classified at the L, CONFIDENTIAL, and SECRET levels"; "Q, TOP SECRET, and SCI levels" |
| Employers request clearances only for jobs that need them, never to stockpile cleared people | same, §117.10(a)(5) | "limit requests … to the minimum number of employees and consultants necessary"; "will not be used to establish a cache of cleared employees" |
| A contractor can request one only for its own employees, so you can't apply on your own | same, §117.10(a)(7) | "Contractors will not submit requests … for individuals who are not their employees or consultants" |
| Citizenship | same, §117.10(k)(1) | "Contractors must make every effort to ensure that non-U.S. citizens are not employed in duties that may require access to classified information." |

## 5. Veteran and military spouse preferences
| Claim | Source | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| Preference applies to new competitive-service appointments and many excepted ones; 5 or 10 points | https://www.opm.gov/fedshirevets/veteran-job-seekers/vets/ | "applies to all new appointments in the competitive service and many in the excepted service"; "an additional 5 or 10 points are added to the numerical score of qualified preference eligible veterans" |
